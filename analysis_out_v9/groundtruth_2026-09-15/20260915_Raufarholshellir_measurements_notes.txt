Raufarhólshellir project
Date 15-09-2026
Researcher: Birgir V. Óskarsson
We measured the roof of the skylights with a measuring tape at two locations, south and north (see map). Some roofs had two inverted terraces, breaking at the joints or contact of a lava, so we measured the uppermost roof and the thicker terrace behind. The lower measurement is perhaps not as accurate, because the height of the tape was estimated by eye from above (and below), but should be close.
