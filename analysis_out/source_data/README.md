# Source data used by the planetary comparison

The third-party documents that were consulted here are not redistributed.
They are:

- Cushing, G. E. (2012). Candidate cave entrances on Mars. Journal of Cave
  and Karst Studies 74(1), 33-47. doi:10.4311/2010EX0167R
- Horvath, T. et al. (2022). Thermal and illumination environments in lunar
  pits and caves. Geophysical Research Letters. doi:10.1029/2022GL099710
- Wagner, R. V. and Robinson, M. S. (2021). Lunar pit morphology. LPSC 52,
  abstract 2530.
- USGS Astrogeology, Mars Global Cave Candidate Catalog v1, archive
  description (https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_global_cave_candidate_catalog_v1_cushing)

The extracted values live in the CSV/JSON files of this directory.
